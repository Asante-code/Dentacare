Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to questions you might ask

How often should i visit the dentist?

For most people, visiting the dentist every six months helps maintain good oral health and allows problems to be detected early.

Does teeth whitening hurt?

Teeth whitening usually doesn’t hurt, but some people may experience temporary tooth sensitivity or mild gum irritation afterward.

What do i do in the case of a dental emergency?

In a dental emergency, stay calm, rinse your mouth gently, avoid touching the affected area, and contact a dentist as soon as possible.
